# KalaSetu — AI Artisan Market Linkage Platform

KalaSetu is an AI-assisted digital marketplace and cataloging platform designed to help marginalized artisans, craft clusters, cooperatives, and rural micro-entrepreneurs connect directly with buyers, institutions, government procurement channels, and bulk demand networks.

The platform combines artisan product publishing, AI-based photo analysis, catalog generation, multilingual user experience, direct WhatsApp buyer contact, order tracking, institutional request handling, and account-based workflows in one unified application.

## Mission

KalaSetu aims to reduce dependency on middlemen and create a transparent, inclusive digital commerce route for artisans. It helps artisans digitize their inventory, optimize product listings using AI, estimate pricing, create multilingual product descriptions, and match demand from retail buyers, corporate purchasers, and government-linked marketplaces.

## Key Features

- AI craft photo analysis and product listing generation
- Artisan studio for uploading, reviewing, and publishing craft products
- Buyer marketplace for catalog browsing and order requests
- Bulk and institutional RFQ workflow for enterprise and government demand
- Unified role profile for buyer and seller workflows
- Wishlist, order history, request history, and account workspace
- Multilingual support across major Indian languages
- Direct WhatsApp buyer connection support
- Notification and account management experience
- GIS-friendly artisan profile and location-aware buyer discovery support
- Admin review and backend-managed institutional request workflows

## Target Users

- Artisans and craft makers
- Local cooperatives and artisan clusters
- Retail and institutional buyers
- Government procurement and public-market linkage teams
- Buyers looking for handmade, rural, and traditional products

## Tech Stack

The project includes a FastAPI backend, SQLite data layer, static web frontend assets, and an Expo/React Native mobile application interface for APK and mobile distribution workflows.

## Outcome

KalaSetu creates a connected ecosystem where artisans can publish craft inventory, understand digital demand, respond to institutional and bulk opportunities, and participate in a fairer national marketplace without requiring advanced technical knowledge.
